# Decision: product name locked — Prantivo

**Date:** 2026-07-24
**Status:** Locked
**Decided by:** Chetan (founder)

## Decision

The product and company name is **Prantivo**, effective immediately. This supersedes
"Zyon" as the working name everywhere it appears: website copy, code identifiers,
docs, and any future Meta/Plivo submissions.

## What this is and isn't

- A **naming decision**, not a legal-registration event. It does not complete business
  registration, domain purchase, or trademark clearance — those stay open, externally-
  clocked action items (`zyon-first-launch-plan.md` Phase 0, Issues 1–2).
- It **does** unblock:
  - WEB-01 Phase 0 STOP condition #1 — session may proceed.
  - Renaming the pervasive "Zyon" display strings in `web/` (`siteConfig.siteName`,
    page-title suffix, footer copyright text, JSON-LD organization name, meta
    descriptions) to "Prantivo." These are brand-display strings, not the legal entity
    name, and need no registration to change. Folded into WEB-01 Part 1 — see the
    updated session file.
- It does **not** unblock:
  - `legalEntityName`, `siteUrl`, registered address, grievance officer, governing-law
    city, contact emails, legal effective dates — still Part 2 of WEB-01, still gated
    on actual entity registration and domain purchase.
  - The registered legal entity name may legitimately differ from the trading name
    "Prantivo" (e.g. a Pvt Ltd legal name with "Prantivo" as brand/trade name) —
    confirm with whoever files the registration; they don't need to match verbatim.

## Quick check performed (not a legal clearance)

- No existing business, product, or trademark called "Prantivo" surfaced in India or
  elsewhere. No conflict found.
- No live site found at a quick search for `prantivo.com` / `.in` — a weak positive
  signal only; this is not a WHOIS lookup. Check availability on a registrar today.
- Incidental: "prantivo" is an existing Portuguese adjective (roughly "mournful,
  plaintive"). Irrelevant to an India-only launch; worth knowing if a Lusophone market
  is ever in scope.

## Still open this week (not blocked on anything above)

- File entity registration under a name your CA confirms can trade as "Prantivo."
